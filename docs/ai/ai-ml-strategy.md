# AI / ML Strategy

## Strategy Overview
The AI verification service acts as an autonomous signal generator that evaluates submitted media against physical reality and task criteria:

1. **Object Detection & Classification:** Confirm requested ground feature (e.g., water source, road obstruction, building damage) exists in photo.
2. **Anti-Spoofing & Authenticity:** Detect re-photographed screens, synthetic/AI-generated imagery, and stock photo reuse.
3. **Temporal & Solar Alignment:** Estimate solar elevation and shadow angles from timestamp/coordinates and verify photo lighting consistency.
4. **Spatial Alignment:** Cross-reference ground imagery perspective with satellite/aerial reference layers.
